# 0.9 Factures Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Legal invoices and credit notes issued from the dossier, on realised hours, split between the contractor and direct-paying funders, numbered continuously, immutable once validated, chained in a tamper-evident journal, rendered to PDF and sent by e-mail.

**Architecture:** New module `Finance` reads Sessions only through a new public service `Sessions\Public\Billing` (dossier price lines, enrolments, fundings, schedule of sessions, realised/absent minutes via `RealisedHours`, abandon rule) and the CRM through `Crm\Public\Fiches`. A pure `BillingCalculator` computes the cumulative billable and each recipient's share at a cut-off; `InvoiceDrafts` turns « due = share − already invoiced » into drafts; `InvoiceValidator` numbers, freezes, hashes and archives. Sessions shows Finance's block on the dossier through a `DossierBlocks` registry.

**Tech Stack:** Laravel 12 / PHP 8.4, PostgreSQL 17 FORCE RLS (triggers for immutability), brick/math, Gotenberg (`Core\Documents\PdfRenderer`), Inertia React, Pest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-0.9-factures-design.md` (§ 4 and § 4 bis are binding).

## Global Constraints

- Money in integer cents with `brick/math` (`RoundingMode::HALF_UP`), never float; minutes as integers; Paris days (`TrainingSession::today()` equivalent in Finance: `CarbonImmutable::now('Europe/Paris')->startOfDay()`).
- Every new table: `tenant_id uuid not null`, `BelongsToTenant`, `Rls::enable`, UUID v7; migrations via `--database=pgsql_owner`.
- Boundaries: Finance never names a Sessions/CRM/Catalogue table (extend `ModuleBoundariesArchTest` with a `SESSIONS_TABLES` pattern for Finance); Sessions, Crm, Catalogue, Core, Connectors never import Finance.
- Numbers: `<prefix>-<AAAA>-<NNNNN>` (5 digits, zero-padded), continuous per (tenant, company, kind, Paris year); defaults prefix `F` (invoice) and `AV` (credit note); allocated only at validation, inside the validation transaction.
- Validated invoice/credit note and its lines: no UPDATE (except `status validated→sent`, `sent_at`, `email_status`, `email_error_code`) and no DELETE, enforced by PostgreSQL triggers; `invoice_journal` append-only (same trigger style as `audit_logs`).
- Hash: SHA-256 hex of the canonical JSON (sorted keys, no whitespace) of the frozen invoice (number, kind, company id, recipient snapshot, dates, lines, totals) concatenated with the previous journal hash of the tenant (`''` for the first).
- Only fundings with status `agreed` and `pays_directly = true` take a share; a funder invoice needs the funding's `agreement_number` to validate (French error « Indiquez le numéro d'accord du financement avant de valider. »).
- VAT: company `vat_regime = exempt` ⇒ VAT 0 and mention « Exonération de TVA, article 261-4-4° du CGI » ; `subject` ⇒ line rate (default 20 %). Dédit line: VAT 0, mention « Indemnité hors champ de la TVA ».
- Fixed line labels: « Dédit — non imputable sur les fonds de la formation professionnelle » ; « Absences non justifiées — non imputables sur les fonds de la formation professionnelle ».
- French UI; CSV `;`; user-facing errors via `UserFacingHttpException` or validation.
- Permissions: `finance.invoices.view`, `finance.invoices.manage`, `finance.invoices.validate`; scope = dossier scope semantics (`own`/`das`/`all` on the invoice's dossier).

## Review Focus

1. Same cut-off computed twice (double click, two users) never produces two drafts/invoices for the same due amount (Task 4 test: second « Préparer » finds nothing due once a draft exists for that recipient and period).
2. A refused or not-yet-agreed funding never reduces the contractor's invoice; becoming agreed later proposes the funder invoice and a matching credit note to the contractor (Task 3/4 tests).
3. Validation racing on two drafts of the same company: numbers consecutive, no gap, no duplicate (Task 5 test with two transactions / sequence lock).
4. A validated invoice cannot be changed through Eloquent, raw SQL or deleted (Task 5 tests hitting the trigger as `tms_app`).
5. Hours entered after an invoice (late attendance) produce the right complement or credit note, never a silent double count (Task 3/4 tests).

### Task 3: BillingCalculator (pure, the core)

**Files:** `tms/app/Modules/Finance/Billing/BillingCalculator.php`, `CutOff.php` (value object: date + month), `Shares.php` (result), tests `tms/tests/Unit/Finance/BillingCalculatorTest.php` and `tms/tests/Feature/Finance/BillingCalculatorFeatureTest.php`.

**Interfaces:**
- `BillingCalculator::cumulative(array $dossier, array $hours, array $plan, string $absencesRule, CutOff $cut): Shares` (pure: arrays from Task 1, no DB).
- `Shares`: `billable_cents` (training part), `dedit_cents`, `absences_cents`, `by_recipient: array<recipientKey, {recipient: {type: contractor|funder, id, funding_id?}, training_cents, dedit_cents, absences_cents, external: bool}>`, `detail: list<{line_id, enrolment_id?, planned, realised, ratio, amount_cents}>` (for display), `ready: bool` + `reason` (e.g. « Mois non clôturé », « Formation non terminée »).
- Rules exactly as spec § 4.3 and § 4 bis: per-trainee lines by enrolment ratio (cap 1), group lines by session ratio, fee lines per rhythm, abandon prorata + dédit (dédit only to contractor), absences (only `bill_contractor`, hourly rate = enrolment price share / planned minutes, contractor only), direct-funder shares (`agreed` + `pays_directly`; targeted fundings follow their target; cap amount; external_billing ⇒ share removed from contractor, `external: true`, never invoiced), schedule (intermediate instalments: contractor share of planned price × cumulative percent; funders only at final realised), planned basis.
- Rounding: compute each line/enrolment amount with HALF_UP to the cent, then sum; shares by proportion HALF_UP; the contractor takes the rounding remainder so the sum equals billable.
